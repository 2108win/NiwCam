## Ghi chú cho người duyệt (App Review Notes, tiếng Anh)

> NiwCam turns the iPhone into a webcam for a Windows PC. It works together with the free NiwCam app for Windows 10/11: https://github.com/2108win/NiwCam/releases/latest (NiwCam-Setup-<version>.exe). No account or sign-in is needed. The interface is in Vietnamese.
>
> How to test:
> 1. Install NiwCam on a Windows PC on the same Wi-Fi network as the iPhone.
> 2. Open NiwCam on the iPhone and allow Camera and Local Network access.
> 3. On the PC, pick the iPhone in the source list. Both devices show the same 6-digit code: confirm it on both.
> 4. The iPhone camera now appears on the PC as the camera "NiwCam" in any app (Windows Camera, Zoom, Teams, Discord, OBS). The PC app controls the lens, zoom, torch, focus and exposure.
> 5. Screen sharing: tap "Chia sẻ màn hình sang máy tính" (Share screen to computer) and start the broadcast. The PC lists "iPhone · Màn hình" as a separate source.
>
> Without a PC the app shows its waiting screen ("Đang chờ máy tính kết nối…", waiting for a computer) with the phone's local address.
>
> Privacy: video goes only to the paired PC over the local network (or a USB cable), encrypted end to end. There are no servers and no data is collected.
